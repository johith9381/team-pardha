Two-Stage Vision Architecture

How Pardha RoadSense Works

Why two steps? Standard object detectors only drop loose square boxes. But a rectangular box around an irregular pothole captures 50% healthy road. We separate localization from metrology.

STEP 01

Fleet Camera Ingestion

Smartphones mounted on municipal buses, delivery fleets, or police patrols stream road frames at 30 FPS alongside synchronized GPS telemetry.

Zero New Sensors
STEP 02

Fast YOLO Detection

A lightweight YOLO detector rapidly screens each frame, outputting spatial candidate boxes for potholes, alligator cracks, and broken shoulders in ~16ms.

High Precision (0.952)
STEP 03

Meta SAM 2 Segmentation

YOLO bounding boxes serve as visual prompts to Meta's Segment Anything Model 2, which traces the exact, irregular contour of the defect with pixel precision.

+12.8% IoU Boost
STEP 04

Metrology & Municipal GIS

Physical area (m²), equivalent diameter, and perimeter are calculated. Combined with road speed and nearby schools, a severity score alerts road authorities.

Automated Dispatch
On-Device Privacy Engine

Vehicle number plates and pedestrians' faces are automatically blurred locally on the device buffer before any frame is stored or transmitted.

Offline-First Edge Sync

Operates seamlessly without 4G/5G connectivity. Detections and GPS tags buffer into a compact local database (under 5MB/day) and sync at transit depots.

Why 0.73 vs 0.86 IoU Matters for Roads

Judges often ask: "Why not just stop at YOLO?" The visual difference below illustrates why accurate outlines are required for repair metrology.

YOLO Box (IoU 0.73)
YOLO Box Only

Captures 45% healthy road. Overestimates asphalt volume. Cannot assess depth or true hazard boundary.

SAM 2 Mask (IoU 0.86)
SAM 2 Micro-Contour

Hugs the exact fracture edge. Computes true surface area (m²), perimeter, and repair urgency accurately.
